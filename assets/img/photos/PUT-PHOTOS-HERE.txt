SIZING
  Export at roughly 2000px on the long edge, JPEG quality ~80. Every slot
  crops with background-size: cover, so the subject wants to be near the
  centre; where it is not, the markup uses a crop hint class
  (.plate__img--top / --upper / --lower / --left / --right).

The site dims and desaturates these on purpose so they sit with the brass and
do not fight the type - do not pre-edit them for that, send them straight.
