Tue

Industry Night

If you work in Greektown, on the casino floor or behind any bar downtown, Tuesday is the one. The room fills late and slow with people who have just finished a shift and have no interest in a queue.

Best night of the week to sit at the rail without planning ahead.

6PM – 2AMService industry

Wed

Midweek Reset

Wednesday is the connoisseur’s night. Enough people to feel like a club, few enough that you can hold a conversation and get a booth on a walk-in.

The kitchen is at its best when it is not slammed — order properly.

6PM – 2AMWalk-in booths

Thu

Downtown Thursday

Detroit’s weekend does not wait for Friday. Thursday brings the bigger rotation, a DJ from nine, and the first wave of bachelor parties coming off Monroe Street after dinner.

Reserve if your group is more than four.

The Main Event

The loudest night on the calendar. Full roster on the stage, rail three deep by ten, and VIP booked out most weeks before the doors even open.

Game Day & Last Light

Doors at eight, every screen on whatever is live. Lions in the fall, Pistons and Wings through the winter, Tigers in the spring — and Ford Field and Comerica are both a short walk away.

The soft landing at the end of a Detroit weekend.

8PM – 2AMAll screens live

02Game Day

Nine minutes from Ford Field.
Eight from Comerica.

When the whistle goes, twenty thousand people walk past the end of our street. We keep the screens on and the kitchen open for the ones who are not ready to go home.

Lions

Sunday doors at eight. Post-game room, every screen, full kitchen. Ford Field is a nine-minute walk down St Antoine.

Tigers

Comerica Park empties straight into Greektown. Weeknight games land right as our doors open at six.

Pistons & Wings

Little Caesars Arena is fourteen minutes on foot or three in a car. Late tip-offs and late faceoffs both finish inside our hours.

Fights & PPV

Big cards go up on the main screens. Call ahead on fight night — booths go early and we will tell you straight if the room is full.

03Occasions

Whatever you are celebrating,
we have seen it.

Bachelor parties

Our bread and butter. Tell us the headcount and the arrival time and we will have a section held, a bottle on ice and a host who knows the groom’s name. Stage announcement included on the larger packages — or skipped entirely, if he would rather it was not.

Birthdays

Same treatment, less sympathy. Booth, bottle, platters from the kitchen, and the room finding out about it whether you like it or not.

Divorce parties

Genuinely one of our most-requested bookings, and we take it as seriously as a wedding. Book the corner section and let someone else run the night.

Team, crew and company nights

Conventions at Huntington Place, crews in from out of town, shop nights and shift parties. We can invoice, split the tab, or take a single card on the door for the whole group.
