03Bottle Service

Put it on the table.

Bottle service runs off your package credit or as a straight order at the table. Everything arrives with mixers, ice and glassware, and your server keeps the table set for as long as you are in it.

How it works

  • Reserve a booth or section — or ask at the door if the room has space
  • Pick from the bottle list when you sit down, not before
  • Your credit covers it; anything over settles at the end of the night
  • Mixers, ice, garnish and glassware are always included
Bottle list and pricing are confirmed at the table on the night — the back bar moves, and we would rather quote you the truth than a web page. Call (313) 964‑5744 for tonight’s list.

04Private Events

Take the whole room.

Mondays we are dark to the public, which makes the entire venue available to you — the floor, the stage, the bar and the kitchen, with your guest list on the door.

01

Corporate & Convention

Crews in for a show at Huntington Place, dealer meetings, shop nights. We can invoice, take a single card, or run a bar package with a hard cap.

02

Big Parties

Thirty to a hundred and fifty. Bachelor, birthday, divorce, reunion — anything where a booth is not going to be enough.

03

Filming & Production

The room is an excellent set and Monday is empty. Location enquiries welcome; we will talk through what is and is not possible with the roster.

What we need from you

  • Date, and a second choice if you have one
  • Headcount, honestly — it changes everything
  • Arrival window and how late you expect to run
  • Whether you want a bar package, an open tab, or a cash bar
  • Anything you want on the stage, or specifically off it

What you get back

  • A written quote, not a verbal one
  • A named contact for the night
  • Door staff briefed on your list
  • Kitchen and bar staffed to your headcount
  • One invoice at the end
