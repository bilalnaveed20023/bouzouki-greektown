03Parking

Four ways to leave the car.

01

Street

Metered on Lafayette, Beaubien and St Antoine. Most Detroit meters stop enforcing in the evening — read the post, it is the cheapest option by far.

02

Casino Deck

The Hollywood Casino structure is the biggest and closest covered option — a two-minute walk and open all night.

03

Greektown Lots

Surface lots and structures off Monroe and Macomb. Flat evening rates most nights, more on game days.

04

Don’t Drive

Rideshare drops at the door on Lafayette. The People Mover’s Greektown station is four minutes away — and if you are drinking, this is the answer.

Game days and event nights change everything about parking downtown. If Ford Field, Comerica or Little Caesars Arena has something on, budget extra time or take a ride.
04House Rules

Six rules. That is all.

They exist to protect the people who work here and the people who come here. Break them and the night ends early — that is the whole policy.

1 · No cameras. Ever.

No photography or video anywhere on the floor, including from a booth, including of your own group. Phones away. This is the rule we enforce hardest.

2 · Do not touch the entertainers.

Unless they initiate it, hands stay to yourself. Ask, do not assume. This is not negotiable and it is not a grey area.

3 · Tip properly.

The stage runs on it and so does the floor. Bring singles.

4 · Management’s call is final.

We reserve the right to refuse entry or service to anyone, for any lawful reason, at any time. Arguing with the door has never once worked.

5 · No drugs, no weapons, no outside alcohol.

Bags may be checked. Anything illegal ends your night and may end it somewhere else.

6 · Look after each other.

If someone in your group is too far gone, we will help you get them a ride. Tell a member of staff — you are not in trouble for asking.
