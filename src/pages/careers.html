Walk-in auditions,
Tuesday to Thursday.

No appointment needed. Come in Tuesday, Wednesday or Thursday from 6:00 PM and ask for the manager at the door. Auditions are on the main stage, one set, and you will have an answer the same night.

What to bring

  • Valid government-issued photo ID proving you are 21 or over
  • Your Social Security card or work authorisation
  • Whatever you would normally wear to work — shoes included
  • Your own music if you have a set you like, otherwise the DJ will run it

Why here

  • The location. Two minutes from Hollywood Casino, eight from Comerica, nine from Ford Field. The traffic walks to us.
  • Nightly payout. No waiting on a cheque.
  • Flexible schedule. Pick your nights; we will work around school, a day job or another house.
  • Real security. Door staff on every shift, a strict no-photography floor, and management that backs you when a guest is out of line.
  • Independent house. You are talking to the people who own it, not a regional office.
