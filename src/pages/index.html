01 The House

A Greektown institution after dark.

Before it was a gentlemen’s club, 432 E Lafayette was a Greek room — bouzouki strings, plates of lamb, belly dancers working the floor between courses. The neighbourhood changed. Detroit changed. The name never did.

Today Bouzouki is what it always was underneath: the room you end up in. A dark, low-lit box in the middle of Greektown where the doors open at four, the stage never really goes quiet, and nobody is looking at the clock.

We are a two-minute walk from Hollywood Casino, five from Comerica Park, ten from Ford Field and Little Caesars Arena. When the game lets out, this is where the night keeps going.
